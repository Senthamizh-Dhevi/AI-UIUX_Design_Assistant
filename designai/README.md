# DesignAI — AI-Powered UI/UX Design Assistant

> Transform simple product ideas into structured, actionable UI/UX design blueprints using Generative AI.

DesignAI is a full-stack AI-powered UI/UX Design Assistant that helps users transform a simple product idea into a structured UX design blueprint and refine it through an interactive AI Design Studio.

The application combines Generative AI, React, Node.js, and Express to provide an AI-assisted product design workflow from idea generation to design refinement, version management, local persistence, and blueprint export.

---

## 📌 Overview

Designing a digital product from scratch involves several stages such as understanding the problem, identifying users, defining features, planning pages, creating user flows, and establishing a design system.

DesignAI simplifies the early product-design process by allowing users to provide a simple product idea and automatically generate a structured UX blueprint.
